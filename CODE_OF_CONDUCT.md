# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in A11y Relay a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of disability, identity, background, experience level, or technology preference.

We welcome contributions from disabled people and expect the project to be built with care, respect, and humility.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Listen to lived experience without demanding personal disclosure.
- Use accessible language and provide context.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and boundaries.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make a reasonable effort to include people using assistive technologies.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Ableist, racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic, or otherwise hateful language.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Pressuring someone to disclose a disability or health information.
- Deliberate disruption of accessibility or safety features.
- Sexualized or unwelcome attention.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers at the contact address listed in the repository. Reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as possible.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.
